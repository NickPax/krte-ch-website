## Positioning

**fx europe's angle: Clutter-free**

Many competitors pile on features (crypto, transfers, calculators, charts). fx europe goes the other way — just currency conversion, done well.

- ✅ **Speed/simplicity** — No bloat, instant results
- ✅ **Offline-first** — Works without connection
- ✅ **Widget** — Check rates without opening app
- ❓ **Privacy** — No account required, no tracking? (confirm)
- ❓ **European focus** — Tailored defaults (EUR, GBP, PLN, CHF)?

**Re: calculator** — Could add as subtle feature (type math in amount field?) without cluttering UI. Worth considering if users request it.
