## Bread Recipe (from Feb 8 conversation)
**Wheat-Rye Bread (Chleb Pszenno-Żytni):**
1. Mix + 360-400ml warm water → knead 10 min → rest 10 min
2. Into greased tin (25×11×8cm) → cover → warm place 1 hour
3. After 30 min: score top
4. Bake 40 min @ 220°C (no fan)
5. Remove from tin immediately
