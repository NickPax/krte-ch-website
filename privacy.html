Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Krte-ch ("we", "our", or "us") operates mobile applications including fx europe and Stampede PDF. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our apps.

Information Collection

Our apps are designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary to provide our services:

  • fx europe: Does not collect any personal data. Exchange rate data is fetched from public APIs.
  • Stampede PDF: All documents are processed locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or have access to your files.

Data Storage

Any data you create within our apps (such as PDF annotations in Stampede PDF) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data and cannot recover it if deleted.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may use third-party services for functionality:

  • Exchange rate APIs for currency conversion (fx europe)
  • App Store / Google Play for distribution and crash reporting

These services may collect anonymous usage data according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
